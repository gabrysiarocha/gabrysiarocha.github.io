about me

Ever since I was young, I have always been creatively inclined. From drawing my favourite characters to experimenting with different art techniques, I have always found joy in expressing my imagination through visual arts.

Now, I've taken that a step further. I have always been interested behind the theory of good web design, and I am currently learning front-end web development, with the goal of creating beautiful and functional websites. Over the past year, I have been developing my skills, and am actively undertaking projects to further develop my expertise.

I am a self-taught web designer, and I am constantly seeking to improve my skills and knowledge. I am passionate about creating websites that are not only visually appealing, but also user-friendly and accessible to all. I believe that good web design is about more than just aesthetics; it's about creating an experience that is intuitive and enjoyable for the user.

While my area of focus is primarily in the visual elements of web design, I know that having the skillset of creating functional websites is just as important, and I am committed to developing my skills in this area with every project or client brief I undertake.

When I'm not designing or coding, you can find me creating art for me and my friends, writing stories, reviewing perfumes, looking at rings in luxury boutiques, baking sugar cookies (my favourite!), or enjoying a good book. Thank you for taking the time to learn more about my journey, and I hope you will follow my progress!
